We sell security assessment. It would be absurd to have no way to receive a report about our own
systems, so here is one.

## In scope

- `attestorsecurity.com` and everything served from it
- `portal.attestorsecurity.com`, the client portal
- Our published email infrastructure and its authentication records

## Out of scope

- **Any system belonging to a client of ours.** If you have found something in a client's system,
  report it to that client. We cannot authorise testing of assets we do not own, and neither can you.
- Third-party services we use, which have their own disclosure programmes
- Findings requiring physical access, social engineering of our staff, or a compromised end-user
  device
- Volumetric or denial-of-service testing of any kind
- Automated scanner output submitted without validation

## How to report

Email <a href={`mailto:${site.securityContactEmail}`}>{site.securityContactEmail}</a> with a
description, the steps to reproduce, and any supporting evidence. Encrypt anything sensitive with the
key published at [PGP KEY URL].

We will acknowledge within [N] business days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.

## What we ask

Test only against accounts you control. Use the minimum access needed to demonstrate the issue. Do
not access, modify or retain anyone else's data — if you reach data that is not yours, stop and tell
us what you reached rather than collecting it. Do not degrade availability, and do not run automated
scans at a rate that affects service. Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before publishing.

## What we offer

We do not currently operate a paid bug bounty and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

We will acknowledge your contribution publicly with your consent, we will tell you what we changed
and when, and we will not pursue legal action in respect of good-faith research conducted in
accordance with this policy.

If you find something in the client portal, say so prominently in your report. That system holds
records of other companies' unfixed vulnerabilities and we treat any finding in it as a release
blocker.
