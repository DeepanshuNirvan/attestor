## What this notice covers

This notice describes how we handle personal data collected through this website.

Personal data we process while performing a security assessment for a client is governed separately
by the data processing agreement with that client. In that context we act as a **data processor** on
the client's instructions, we do not decide the purposes of the processing, and the safeguards are
contractual rather than described here.

## What we collect

If you submit the contact form, we collect the name, work email address, company name and message
you provide. Nothing else. There are no file uploads on this website.

If you book a call, our scheduling provider collects the details you enter into its own form.

Our hosting and analytics providers process technical information such as approximate location,
browser type and pages viewed, in aggregate. We do not use advertising trackers, we do not build
profiles across sites, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising. There is no Google
Analytics on this site and there will not be.

## Why we process it

To respond to your enquiry, to prepare a proposal, and to keep a record of business correspondence.
The basis is your consent when you submit the form, together with our legitimate interest in
responding to enquiries about our services.

## Cookies

We use only what is necessary to operate the site. We set no advertising or profiling cookies. The
form challenge (Cloudflare Turnstile) is used to keep automated submissions out and does not track
you across sites.

## Retention

Enquiry correspondence is retained for [ENQUIRY RETENTION PERIOD] and then deleted, unless it forms
part of a client engagement record, which is retained in line with our contractual and statutory
obligations.

Testing evidence from an engagement is a separate matter and is deleted {site.evidenceRetentionDays}
days after the report is released, with a written deletion confirmation issued to the client.

## Sharing

We use service providers for hosting, form delivery, email and scheduling. They process data on our
instructions and are bound by confidentiality. We do not otherwise disclose your data except where
required by law.

We do not send client data, evidence or credentials to any third-party artificial intelligence
service. Where a client explicitly enables AI assistance for their own engagement, only redacted
findings text is sent — never raw evidence, never credentials, never their customers' data.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, or
nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity,
in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it. Write
to <a href={`mailto:${site.contactEmail}`}>{site.contactEmail}</a>.

If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, equivalent rights apply and we will
honour them.

## Security

We apply access control, encryption in transit and at rest, and least privilege. The marketing
website has no database, no login and no administrative interface, and accepts no file uploads. Our
internal platform is not reachable from the public internet.
