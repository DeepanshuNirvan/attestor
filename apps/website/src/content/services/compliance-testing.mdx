Most companies buy a security test because a date, a contract or a deal requires one. This service
is for that case. The testing is the same as any other engagement. What changes is that the report
is shaped so your auditor accepts it without a second round.

## What each framework asks for

**ISO 27001:2022.** Control A.8.8 requires that technical vulnerabilities are identified, evaluated
and fixed, with dates. An auditor wants to see the finding, the decision, the fix and the
verification. That is the assessment-then-retest cycle, so here the retest report is the evidence,
not an extra.

**SOC 2.** CC7.1 asks for evidence that you identify vulnerabilities. CC6.1 asks for evidence that
access controls restrict access to authorised users. We write the access control matrix section of
the report so you can hand it over for CC6.1 as it is.

**PCI DSS 4.0.** Requirement 11.4 asks for an annual external and internal penetration test.
Requirement 11.4.4 asks for evidence that exploitable findings were corrected and testing repeated.
Without a retest report, 11.4.4 is not met.

**India's DPDP Act 2023.** The Act requires reasonable security safeguards. There is no certificate
and no prescribed test, so what you need is evidence that you looked, found things and fixed them.
The rules were notified in November 2025 and the compliance date is May 2027. Building the evidence
trail now is cheaper than assembling one later.

## What we do not do

We do not certify you. We are not an accreditation body and we are not CERT-In empanelled. A report
claiming to certify compliance would be worth nothing to your auditor.

Your report includes a mapping appendix showing which findings touch which control. It states
plainly that it is a mapping for your convenience and not a compliance opinion.

## Working with your compliance consultant

If you already have an ISO or SOC 2 consultant, introduce us. They sell the certificate and run the
programme. They usually cannot do the technical test, and the auditor requires one.

We are happy to be the technical half of that, including speaking to your auditor directly about
methodology, so you do not have to translate between us.

## If you are a regulated entity

If you are regulated by the RBI, SEBI or IRDAI, your statutory audit has to be done by a CERT-In
empanelled organisation, and we are not one. We will say so on the first call rather than after you
have paid.

We can do the testing beforehand and verify your fixes, so that audit passes first time. That is
usually cheaper than failing it once.
