We test the application the way someone with a login would attack it, not the way a scanner crawls
it.

An unauthenticated scan finds missing headers and out-of-date libraries. It does not find that your
support role can read an enterprise customer's invoices, that the checkout accepts the total sent by
the browser, or that a coupon can be used twice if two requests arrive at the same moment. Finding
those needs a tester logged in as two different users at once, comparing what each one can reach.

## How the five days are spent

**Day zero is machine time.** Discovery, crawling, the tool suite and evidence capture all run
unattended, inside your agreed window and rate limits. This produces a queue of candidates, not
findings.

**Day one is triage.** A tester reproduces every candidate by hand or discards it. Tools in this
category report 40 to 70% false positives, and we do not pass those on to you.

**Days one to four are the testing.** Access control across every pair of roles, in both directions:
one role against another, and one customer against another customer in the same role. Business
logic: workflow order, price and quantity handling, and race conditions on anything with a limit.
Multi-step flows, password reset and session handling. Then we work out what several small findings
add up to when combined, and write that up as a narrative with a business outcome at the end.

**Day five is the report**, plus a thirty-minute call with the people who will do the fixing.

## What we need from you

**Two accounts per role.** Half of access control testing is checking whether customer A can reach
customer B's data, which is impossible with one account per role. If your application has customers,
admins and support staff, that is six test accounts. They can be disposable, and they should be. We
will ask you to rotate them when we finish.

**A staging environment that matches production**, if you have one. If we have to test production,
tell us early. We halve every rate limit, suppress state-changing requests on the first pass, and
agree a contact who can stop the test and is reachable throughout.

## When to buy the complex tier instead

Buy the complex tier if you have four or more roles, multiple tenants, payment or approval
workflows, or an application too large to cover in five days. It runs for eight days and adds
tenant isolation testing across separate customer organisations, settlement and approval workflow
testing, and a longer phase on chained findings.

We will tell you on the scoping call which tier we think you need, including when that is the
cheaper one.
